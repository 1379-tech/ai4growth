About Ai4Growth

Ai4Growth was built to solve a simple problem: businesses are already generating demand, but they are not converting it effectively.

Missed calls, slow replies, and inconsistent follow-up result in lost revenue every day. Most businesses don’t realise how much opportunity is slipping through these gaps.

Ai4Growth installs systems that capture every enquiry, respond instantly, and follow up automatically—so more conversations turn into booked work.

About the founder

Mark Whitworth works directly with business owners to identify where revenue is being lost and implement practical systems that improve response speed, follow-up, and conversion.

The focus is always measurable outcomes: more booked jobs, better client retention, and increased revenue from existing demand.
